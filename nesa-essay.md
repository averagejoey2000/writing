I tell people "The small boat sailing merit badge ruined my life." 
It's clickbait, I know, but that day in Summer Camp, in Mission Bay San Diego, at Camp Fiesta Island, set my life on the path to the career I am now in.
My parents at first wanted me to be a Doctor, a Lawyer, a Banker, or something else so high paying.
I think I was 13 that year, and we had record breaking heat in Las Vegas, so we were very glad to get to go to an aquatics based camp.
I took the small boat sailing merit badge; on Monday and Tuesday, we just talked about how to operate the boats, but on Wednesday we got to go out for the first time.
I remember my buddy in the boat fell out and I had to come back to recover him, and that was the first time I had both the tiller and the sheet in my hand; it was a breezy day and I felt so powerful, like I was wrestling with Mother Nature.

I joined the sea scouts when I turned 14, and every time I went to a Summer camp I always took all the aquatics merit badges.
My first paying job was as a counselor for Emerald Bay and an Aquatics/Sailing instructor.
That's also where I found out about Cal Maritime, and I applied for the following fall semester and was admitted in 2020 to the class of 2024.
Now I'm in my first job as a cut loose 3rd Officer, and I get to sail resupply vessels in support of the Navy.
None of that would have happened if I didn't get in a boat that hot July over 10 years ago.
